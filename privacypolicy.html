Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

At Brews & Bytes, accessible from 123 Paul Kruger Street, Pretoria City, or through our website, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by Brews & Bytes and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

When you reserve a workspace, book a Silent Focus Pod, or submit an inquiry through our contact form, we may collect personal information including:

  • Your full name
  • Email address and contact details
  • Workspace booking preferences, dates, and times
  • Any message or feedback details sent to us

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information in various ways, including to:

  • Process and manage your workspace and pod reservations
  • Respond directly to your inquiries and support requests
  • Improve our website, amenities, and café services
  • Send confirmation updates or important notices regarding your bookings

3. On-Site Network & Wi-Fi Usage

When connecting to our high-speed Wi-Fi or Ethernet ports at our Pretoria location, basic device telemetry (such as MAC addresses and session duration) may be processed solely for network security, bandwidth allocation, and infrastructure maintenance.

4. Data Security

We value your trust in providing us with your personal information and implement reasonable technical measures to protect it. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.
